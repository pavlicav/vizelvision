Live transcribed captions

Whisper transcribes the sound locally before broadcast. Captions appear for every viewer, above the crawl, timed to the speech. Captions and ad detection share one audio/video delay. Automatic captions can make mistakes.

Tiny is faster; Small favors accuracy. Models ending in .en are English only. Other models can detect or transcribe the selected language.

Station ID

Last ID on air

How the ID works

  • A 30-second ID plays after every program in Up next. If another program is next, it replaces the previous program's last 30 seconds instead, so scheduled starts are never delayed.
  • It holds its end card (the “STAY TUNED” lockup at 27.9 s) while the next source is prepared, so a channel change never shows the fallback card.
  • Before a scheduled game, the Sports ID (“The following is a presentation of VizelVision Sports”) plays instead, announcements on or off.
  • With continuity on, the announcer's lines are mixed over the ID's music bed at −20 LUFS. A voice or mix failure falls back to the plain ID.
  • Skipped IDs are logged (“Station ID skipped: …” in the web log).
  • Built by ident/build.py; the sound family is on the Sonic identity sheet.
