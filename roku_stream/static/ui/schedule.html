    How it works · what airs when
    Who gets master, in order
    1. A game holds master until it is final. Never cut early, even past its window; estimated from the live inning or quarter.
    2. Then a game already under way (another one that started meanwhile).
    3. Then recordings waiting in Up next, until the next live show starts.
    4. Then the schedule: scheduled shows, else the painted grid, else OFF AIR.
    Games follow the sports feeds

    Until a game starts, its show follows MLB Stats and ESPN every 10 minutes: a moved game takes its whole window along (pregame lead kept), the title follows a decided matchup unless you renamed it, and a game missing for 20 minutes or cancelled is removed and listed below. A feed outage changes nothing.

    The overlay on the grid is GET /v1/playback: Up next and the schedule played forward under these rules. Blocks airing as painted are not drawn again.

        How the backup works

        When the game is final (MLB Stats API; ESPN for the other leagues), Return to schedule airs whatever the grid and shows have, unless a game under way or recordings in Up next come first; the next live show still starts on time. A backup program airs until the window ends, which ends early where another show starts. A game running past its window stays on air, and the backup is skipped if it ends after the window.

        Priority decides overlaps. A show that outranks what is on air cuts in at its start, even into a game before its final or a take held to an end time (those are Normal). Otherwise what is on air keeps master, and the show joins in progress when it ends.
