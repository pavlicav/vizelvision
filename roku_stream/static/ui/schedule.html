        How it works · what airs when
        Who gets master, in order
        1. A game holds master until it is final. Only a show or game of higher priority cuts in.
        2. Then a game already under way (one that started meanwhile joins in progress).
        3. Then recordings waiting in Up next, until the next live show starts.
        4. Then the schedule: shows on a date, else the lineup's slots, else OFF AIR.
        Slots, listings and sports

        A slot that follows a programme looks it up in the listings (GET /v1/programming/search) for each airing, and is settled 15 minutes before it starts. The listings reach a day or two; past that, its usual channel is assumed. Sports slots and favorites are checked every 10 minutes, and scheduled games follow MLB Stats and ESPN until they start: a moved game takes its window along, and one missing for 20 minutes is removed.
