Sonic Identity

How VizelVision sounds: a nineties station on a warm night at the ballpark. One four-note logo, one lift, one clap, played on brass, bells and a stadium organ, through a little tape grit. Every promo, bumper and ID draws from this sheet.

The mnemonic

The logo: Viz-el-Vi-sion

Four notes in the rhythm of the name: eighth, eighth, quarter, half. It has two forms. The open form asks, "there's more", and the closed form answers, "here it is".

Signature devices

Four moves that say VizelVision

The lift

C → D

A key change is always up a whole step, the old broadcast modulation. The logo's closed form is the lift made melody.

The clap

The logo's rhythm, clapped by a crowd or played on a snare. It carries the brand where no melody fits, like crowd beds and score stings.

The channel change

A click and half a second of static open the ID and can open any bumper. It is the station taking the air.

The button

Every piece ends on a hit on its last note, rings out, then gives half a second of silence. That tail makes any cut out of it clean.

Instruments and texture

The palette

Ballpark SPORTS

  • Stadium organTonewheel drawbars (16′ 8′ 4′), Leslie swirl, a little overdrive. Calls and comping.
  • CrowdA murmur, swells to a roar, and the clap. Always under, never the lead.
  • DrumlineSnare cadences with flams, bass drum on one and three.
  • Stadium hornLow detuned saws. For finals only.

Broadcast NETWORK

  • Brass sectionStabs and fanfares; the logo's body.
  • FM bellsThe logo's shine, doubled an octave up.
  • Timpani and orchestra hitThe button and the big moment.
  • "Aah" choirThe closed logo only.

Studio 1990

  • DX e-pianoChords under voices.
  • Synth bassRoot-and-octave, pushing the beat.
  • Gated snareThe eighties-into-nineties room, cut off hard.

  • DustSparse clicks and a hiss floor, in beds only.

Keep it recognizable

Rules

Do

  • Keep the logo's intervals and rhythm exact. Transpose only by the lift.
  • End every piece on the open or closed logo, then the half second of silence.
  • Put the brand in the clap when there's no room for melody.
  • Keep grit to tape plus one other texture at a time.
  • Build from ident/sonic.py and ident/jingle.py, so the instruments stay one family.

Don't

  • Don't set the logo in a minor key or resolve the open form's sixth.
  • Don't play music under live game audio. Announcements go over the ID, never over the game.
  • Don't use the horn or the choir outside finals and the closed logo; they lose their weight.
  • Don't borrow stadium chants or other teams' calls. The organ calls with the logo.
  • Don't exceed −20 LUFS to "cut through". The whole station sits at one level.
