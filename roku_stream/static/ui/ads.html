      How it works

      Watch, weigh, err towards sound, fix, learn
      1. It watches and listens ahead of you. Master holds the programme a few seconds (6 s with captions on); in that time the detector sees each cut and black frame, hears the words (Whisper) and fingerprints the sound before you do.
      2. It knows the graphics. A score bug or a network logo where it sits (NBC's and FOX's MLB score bugs, FOX's bug, NBC's Sunday night ones, and any it learns on a channel: up in the programme, down in its breaks, and still up across the programme's cuts) means the programme: no mute starts under a score bug. YouTube TV's "Sponsored" label means an ad: it mutes at once. Tickers that run through the breaks never count, and a graphic seen up during a known ad (or a label during the programme) stops counting.
      3. It weighs the evidence. The station or score bug, cutting, whether this looks like the programme so far, the words, and above all whether the sound is an ad it has heard before. A mute starts at a black frame, or at a hard cut when what follows is clearly an ad (stations like WJLA fade to black at every break; cable feeds often cut straight in), in games wherever it is sure, or on a known ad.
      4. It errs towards sound. A mute must be confirmed within 10 s or it is taken back, and the sound returns once the detector is no longer sure and what it has already seen ahead agrees, so a mute doesn't cut in and out. Early in an MLB inning break, only a clear cut back to the game ends it. Mutes are capped, master brings the sound back if the detector stops answering, and with the detector down nothing is muted. During an MLB game, play in progress is never muted.
      5. You fix it in one press. "It's the show" (u) and "It's an ad" (a), here or on On Air, act at once on the picture shown; the marks and fixes are kept as training data.
      6. It learns ads. A break it confirms teaches it that break's sound (an ad counts once heard in two breaks); an MLB game's breaks are learnt from the play-by-play as the game goes; recordings can be added with admute.seed. Show sound that repeats, like a theme, is never learnt as an ad, and "It's the show", "Was the show" or Forget (above) removes one at once.
