How it works · the signal pathlive

A source is prepared first (tuned and verified while master still shows what is on air), then taken to master in one cut. Master mixes in the station clock, the crawl and the station ID, and the publisher sends one stream to every viewer. Lag is how far master's output runs behind its input.
